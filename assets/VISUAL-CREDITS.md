### Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated transparent PNG sprite for a peaceful 16th-century sailing exploration web game. Create ONE exquisitely painted small Portuguese-inspired merchant sailing caravel seen STRICTLY FROM DIRECTLY OVERHEAD, orthographic top-down, bow pointing straight to the TOP of the image, stern toward bottom. Ship aligned vertical center, entire ship including spars and bowsprit visible, generous transparent padding. Genuine transparent alpha background, no sea, no water, no backdrop, no frame, no ground shadow, no text, no watermark. Warm semi-realistic hand-painted strategy game illustration, lovely premium miniature craftsmanship, richly textured honey teak hull, visible deck boards and stern cabin, brass trim, coils of rope, two masts with full ivory canvas square sails spread across the width with natural curved folds and stitched panels, small triangular foresail and modest rust-red pennant. Sails should not cover the whole ship; expose wood deck along the sides and stern. Hull long and slim, about 2.7 times longer than the hull width; sails 1.5 times hull width. Gentle overhead diffuse warm sunlight, clear silhouette and legible at 120px high. No weapons, no cannons, no skulls, no characters. NOT isometric, NOT side view, NOT diagonal, NOT a photographic boat. Only this single north-facing boat sprite on transparent background.
